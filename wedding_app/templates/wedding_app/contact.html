Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our wedding invitations

We recommend ordering your wedding invitations 4-6 months before your wedding date. This allows time for design, revisions, printing, and mailing. Invitations should ideally be sent to guests 6-8 weeks before the wedding, or 3 months in advance for destination weddings.

Yes! We love creating custom designs that perfectly reflect each couple's unique style and wedding vision. Our design team will work closely with you to create invitations that are completely personalized. Contact us to discuss your ideas and get started on a custom design.

A complete invitation suite typically includes the main invitation, RSVP card with envelope, details/information card, and outer envelope. Depending on your needs, you might also want to include maps, accommodation information, rehearsal dinner invitations, or reception cards. We can help you determine what pieces you need based on your wedding specifics.

Yes, we offer both traditional printed invitations and digital options. Our digital invitations can be shared via email or text and include interactive RSVP features. They're a great eco-friendly and budget-conscious alternative to printed invitations, while still maintaining beautiful design elements.

Our pricing varies based on design complexity, print methods, paper quality, quantity needed, and additional customizations. Basic invitation suites start at $5 per set, while premium options with special printing techniques like letterpress or foil stamping range from $10-$20 per set. Contact us for a personalized quote based on your specific requirements.
